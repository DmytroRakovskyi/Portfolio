01 / About

Every release is a promise to users

My job is to make sure we keep it. For 8+ years I've stood between the team that builds a product and the people who rely on it, across AAA games, live-casino iGaming, web and mobile. I've led a QA team, and today I test both backend and frontend as a Senior QA Engineer.

In practice that means questioning requirements before they turn into rework, putting testing effort where the real risk is, and giving the team a straight answer to "are we ready?". I don't stop at reporting a problem. I help solve it.

I use AI as a team of agents with their own skills, workflows and memory, and I work a lot with MCP to connect them to the systems a tester uses every day. That's how I write my own tools and automation, and I teach colleagues to do the same. I know how prompting works, follow new models closely and keep practising at home on pet projects. I'm also studying system design.

Quality isn't added at the end. It's built in every conversation, every decision and every release. That's the part of the job I enjoy most.
